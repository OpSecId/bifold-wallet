Intent

Ridge is a holder UI, not a marketing site. The person on the other side of the glass is unlocking a credential, scanning a QR, or sharing a proof. Surfaces stay matte and quiet — anodized metal, not glass. Orange is an action, never decoration.

Signal is scarce

Use orange only for the primary action, the Scan node, and a verified contact. If everything is orange, nothing is urgent.

Core labels data

The muted sage from the fingerprint core is for meta labels: CREDENTIAL OFFER, VERSION, PIN. Never for buttons.

One signature: the node

A 6px Signal dot is the circuit contact from the mark. It means “this is live / verified.” The Scan control is the same idea at 72px.

Rounded, not soft

Cards 20, controls 16. Hairline inner edge, no drop shadow. Depth comes from graphite on ink, not glow.

Wait in motion

DIDComm is slow. The UI is not. Three Signal nodes pulse while the agent works. Never a frozen spinner.

Color

Near-black field, grey metal stacks, OpSecId orange sampled from the official icon.

Type

Outfit for everything a person reads as UI. IBM Plex Mono for anything that is a secret, an identifier, or a machine value.

Voice

Sentence case. Name the thing the person is doing. Errors say what failed and what to do next. Empty states invite an action.

Do

Scan to get started. Accept credential. Unlock failed — try again. No cards yet.

Don’t

Welcome to your secure digital identity journey. Submit. Oops, something went wrong.

Motion

Feedback is faster than the network. A tap scales in 90ms. Screens rise 18pt in 220ms. While DIDComm talks to a mediator, three Signal nodes pulse so the pause never feels stuck. Perceived progress runs ahead, then crawls until the record lands.
